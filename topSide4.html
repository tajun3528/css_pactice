Quick care guide

Three habits for happy plants.

How often should I water?

Check the top 2 cm of soil. If it feels dry, water thoroughly until it drains. Most plants prefer less water in winter.

How much light do they need?

Bright, indirect light suits most houseplants. A spot near a window, out of harsh midday sun, works well.

When should I repot?

Repot every 1 to 2 years, or when roots poke out of the drainage holes. Pick a pot one size larger.
